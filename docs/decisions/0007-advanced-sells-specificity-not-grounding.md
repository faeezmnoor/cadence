# Advanced research sells specificity + fit, not grounding

Former id: D-007 (retired Notion log). Original status line: accepted. Original date line: 2026-06-14.

## Context and problem statement
The 2026-06 eval campaign tested whether Advanced grounds better than Standard. It does **not** — grounding tied at the judge's floor (~2.3 vs ~2.4), because authoritative MY gov/data sources are JS-rendered and unfetchable for every stack.

## Considered options
- A. Sell Advanced as better grounded.
- B. Sell Advanced on specificity and fit (chosen).

## Decision outcome
Advanced's measured premium is **specificity + fit** (denser, more specific, better-tailored briefs). **Honesty rule:** user-facing copy must NOT sell Advanced as better-sourced, more-grounded, "deep research", or "cross-checked".

## Consequences
COPY_GUIDE bans those claims (enforced by the copy-honesty pass, CAD-234). Closing the grounding gap is open product work, not a shipped claim.
